Ask a Specialist

How do I register to bid at an auction?

Registration is free and takes only a few minutes. Simply create an account online and complete a short verification process. Once approved, you will be able to place bids in our auctions.

What is the buyer’s premium?

The buyer’s premium is an additional fee applied to the final hammer price of each lot. The exact percentage may vary depending on the auction and will always be clearly indicated before you place a bid.

Can I bid online, by telephone, or in absentia?

Yes. You can participate in our auctions online in real time, leave absentee bids in advance, or request telephone bidding for selected lots.

How do I sell a work through Haus Rhein?

To consign a work, simply contact our specialists or submit images and details online. We will provide an initial evaluation and guide you through the consignment process.

When and how do I pay after winning a lot?

Payment is typically required within a few days after the auction. We accept bank transfers and other standard payment methods. Full details will be provided on your invoice.

Do all lots have a reserve price?

Most lots are offered with a confidential reserve price agreed with the seller. This ensures that works are not sold below an acceptable level.

How does shipping and collection work?

After payment, you may either collect your purchase or request assistance with shipping. We can recommend trusted shipping partners and coordinate delivery worldwide.
